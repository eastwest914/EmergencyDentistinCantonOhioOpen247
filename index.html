Emergency Dentist 24/7 Canton is a dental office built around urgent care. We focus on the problems that cannot wait: sudden pain, swelling, broken or knocked-out teeth and infections that need prompt attention.

What Happens When You Call

A member of our team will ask about your symptoms, give you advice for managing pain until you arrive and set up your visit. At the office we will examine the tooth, take any images needed and talk through your treatment options before anything begins.

Emergency Dental FAQ

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, swelling in the face or gums, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, a lost filling or crown with pain and signs of infection are all reasons to call right away.


Can I be seen the same day?

We do our best to offer same-day dentist appointments for urgent problems. Call us as soon as you can so we can arrange the earliest visit.


Are you really open at night and on weekends?

Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week.


Will I need a root canal or an extraction?

It depends on what the exam shows. We will explain whether the tooth can be saved and what each option involves before we start treatment.


How can I pay?

We accept cash and credit cards. We will go over costs with you before treatment so you know what to expect.
